Keepr privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026
Applies to Keepr 0.8 and later. Earlier versions export unencrypted ZIP backups.
Developer: deborahfam — https://github.com/deborahfam

Data on your device

Keepr stores saved links, shared text, captions, photos, contact details, notes, categories and preferences in app-private storage. Keepr has no developer-operated backend, accounts, advertising or analytics. It does not request your device address book or location. Locations and contact details are parsed from content you save.

Network requests

Saving or retrying a link can download previews and photos, including in background tasks. Source websites and image hosts receive your IP address, requested URL (including any query parameters) and normal request headers. Keepr does not sign into social accounts or send your collection to the developer. Preview requests do not use account cookies or authorization headers. Unencrypted HTTP preview downloads are blocked; a saved HTTP link may have no preview.

Update checks and external links

An update check contacts GitHub only when you request it. GitHub receives your IP address and request headers, but no saved posts. Opening links, the creator profile, contacting a saved contact or downloading an APK uses an external app governed by its own privacy policy.

Backups and sharing

Android automatic backup and device transfer are disabled for Keepr data. New manual backups are password-encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM and a password-derived key. Keepr does not store or recover your backup password. Older ZIP backups and shared post files are NOT encrypted. A backup includes saved content, notes, contacts and Trash. A destination you choose may be a cloud storage provider. Shared titles, links and photos can contain personal information even with optional fields disabled. Review content and recipients before sharing. Recipients can retain copies. Keepr cannot delete copies held by recipients or storage providers.

Retention and deletion

Posts moved to Trash are retained for the selected 7, 30 or 90 days (30 by default). Cleanup runs when Keepr/Trash opens or when Android schedules the cleanup task. You can permanently delete posts from Trash. Temporary shared exports may remain in the app cache; old exports are cleaned when preparing a new file. Legacy migration recovery copies are removed after a successful database migration. Temporary restore files may remain after an interrupted process until the next app start. To remove all local app data, clear Keepr storage in Android Settings or uninstall. Delete exported backups and recipient copies separately. Keepr does not promise forensic erasure from device storage.
